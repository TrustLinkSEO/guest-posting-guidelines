# Guest Posting Guidelines

TrustLinkSEO supports content placement and publisher outreach opportunities for businesses and digital marketing agencies. Availability and suitability depend on the website, topic, and campaign requirements.

## Our Content Principles

Content should be:

- Original and written for people first.
- Relevant to the publication's audience and subject matter.
- Clear, accurate, and professionally edited.
- Useful to readers rather than created solely to insert links.
- Free from plagiarism, misleading claims, and unnecessary keyword stuffing.

## Publisher Evaluation Checklist

Before considering a website, review:

1. **Topical relevance:** Does the site cover topics related to the campaign?
2. **Audience fit:** Is its content useful to the intended readers?
3. **Editorial quality:** Are articles edited and presented consistently?
4. **Authenticity:** Does the website appear to publish genuine, useful material?
5. **Transparency:** Are commercial relationships and paid placements handled appropriately?
6. **Link policy:** Are link attributes and disclosures consistent with applicable search engine guidelines?

## Suggested Submission Details

When discussing a guest post opportunity, prepare:

- Target topic and intended audience
- Proposed title or outline
- Draft or content brief
- Author or business information
- Any proposed references or links
- Preferred publication timeframe

## Editorial Review Process

1. Share the topic and campaign requirements.
2. Check relevance and publisher availability.
3. Prepare or review the content against editorial standards.
4. Confirm publication details and any applicable disclosures.
5. Review the live page and document the outcome.

## Important Notes

Acceptance, publication dates, placement availability, and search performance cannot be guaranteed. Paid or sponsored links should be disclosed and qualified in line with applicable platform and search engine policies.
